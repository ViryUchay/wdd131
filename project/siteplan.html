HelmetCrest – Website Site Plan

Your One-Stop Fix-It-All Company

Site Purpose

HelmetCrest provides fast and reliable repair services for homes, electronics, vehicles, and general maintenance. The website will allow customers to explore services, read testimonials, view pricing, learn DIY tips, and book repair appointments online.

Target Audience

  • Homeowners needing repairs
  • Busy professionals seeking quick service
  • Vehicle owners
  • DIY learners

Site Map

  • Home (index.html)
  • Services (services.html)
  • Contact / Booking (contact.html)
  • References (references.html)
